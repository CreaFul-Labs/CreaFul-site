Advancing Muscle Preservation for the GLP-1 Era and Healthy Aging

The Challenge

Muscle loss is an increasingly important health concern among individuals using GLP-1 therapies, women navigating midlife transitions, and aging populations.

Our Approach

We are exploring novel creatine-carnosine conjugates that bring together established components of muscle physiology within a single molecular platform.

Our Vision

Build the next generation of muscle-preservation technologies designed for metabolic health, healthy aging, and long-term quality of life.
